At first glance, reading the Amazon sample

This book is right up my alley. It covers wills and trusts, choosing optimism, taking control of your own destiny and happiness, and smiling along.

At the same time, it appears highly personal. This is for you and me. What? But now, today, we're talking at the Marin Philosophical Society, and I wonder what the book thinks about, or feels about, or mentions: ethics and morality, living in society, and being with other people.

Are people just a way of stopping loneliness, or is there some meaning to be found in the lives of others?

It appears that the book primarily focuses on individual well-being and personal optimism, but I am curious to see how it addresses our responsibilities and connections to others in society.

And I ask this as I sit here in a meeting of the Marin Philosophical Society, and not sitting in a room full of old people watching a Buck Institute video.

I am interested in how the principles of optimism and personal well-being intersect with our ethical obligations and social responsibilities. Can one truly maintain optimism while ignoring the broader societal context, or what is balance to be struck?

Where do his principles live within the spectrum of hedonism and eudaimonia?

## Shigehiro-Oishi

* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigehiro_Oishi
* Life in three dimensions.

Happiness and well-being are influenced by personal, relational, and societal factors, highlighting the importance of considering multiple dimensions of life satisfaction.

Shigehiro Oishi is a social psychologist known for his research on happiness, well-being, and the role of social relationships in life satisfaction. His work often explores how our connections with others influence our overall happiness and how social context shapes our experiences of well-being.

## Seligman & Christopher Peterson (psychologist)

* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_psychology

Martin Seligman and Christopher Peterson are prominent figures in the field of positive psychology. Their work focuses on understanding and promoting human strengths, virtues, and well-being. They emphasize the importance of cultivating positive emotions, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishments (often referred to as the PERMA model) to enhance overall life satisfaction and happiness.

## Martha Nussbaum

Martha Nussbaum is a contemporary philosopher known for her work in political philosophy, ethics, and the philosophy of emotions. She has made significant contributions to the development of the capability approach, which focuses on what individuals are able to do and be, emphasizing human flourishing and social justice.
