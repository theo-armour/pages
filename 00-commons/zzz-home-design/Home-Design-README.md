## 2026-08-07 Memory Palace: Everything Visible

You can see everything you might ever need Without leaving a finger, sitting wherever you are.

From David Allen, GTD: Get it out of your brain. Use everything visible in memory palace. This means you don't have to keep a memory of everything in your own brain, cluttering up your brain. Everything visible becomes your external brain.

Can these become tips and tricks for successful aging? We shall see.

### Why should everything be visible?

### Why does everything visible save energy?

Getting things takes effort.
If you can see everything, you are less likely to make a mistake and go into the wrong place.

### How can Everything visible. Stop hoarding.

### How can everything visible make you happier?

Things you love dearly are not hidden away.

### How can everything visible reduce stress and anger?

If you know your drawer is a full sink with things you don't need, this can be stressful!

If you see the things you don't like, it's easier to say, "Oh, let me throw that one out."
