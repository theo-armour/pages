# Self-Representation Journal

## 2026-10-07

The last person I would want to speak to is a thing that acts, looks, and speaks like me. What could I learn from it?

I think it would be much more interesting to talk to a third person, a narrator, an observer. Somebody who knows me intimately, knows all my ins and outs, but could reflect, explain, and question who I am 

My alter ego, my mini-me, my other self, but not my doppelganger.
