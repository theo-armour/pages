# Survey: 82% Of Americans Say They Live On ‘Autopilot’

* https://studyfinds.com/americans-live-on-autopilot/
* Average American energy starts fading at 2:48 p.m., with 21% saying it drops before noon.
* Eighty-two percent say they run their lives on “autopilot,” and 71% skip or delay meals because they are too busy, an average of 3.4 times a week.
* Caffeine is the more common busy-day pick (29%) over a high-protein snack (21%), yet 29% of caffeine drinkers rate it “very effective” compared with 38% of those choosing protein.
