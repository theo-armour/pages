# Shigehiro-Oishi

* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigehiro_Oishi

Shigehiro Oishi is a social psychologist known for his research on happiness, well-being, and the role of social relationships in life satisfaction. His work often explores how our connections with others influence our overall happiness and how social context shapes our experiences of well-being.

## Life in Three Dimensions

Oishi's concept of "Life in Three Dimensions" emphasizes the importance of considering multiple aspects of our lives to achieve overall well-being. These dimensions typically include:
* The personal dimension: This includes individual well-being, personal goals, and self-care practices that contribute to one's overall happiness.
* The relational dimension: This focuses on the quality of social relationships, including family, friends, and community connections, which are crucial for life satisfaction.
* The societal dimension: This considers one's role and engagement within the broader society, including social responsibilities, ethical behavior, and contributions to the community.

Oishi argues that a balanced and fulfilling life requires attention to all three dimensions, as neglecting any one of them can undermine overall well-being. By integrating personal, relational, and societal considerations, individuals can achieve a more comprehensive and sustainable sense of happiness and life satisfaction.
