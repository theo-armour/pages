# Susan Neiman

* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Neiman

>Susan Neiman (/ˈnaɪmən/;[1] born March 27, 1955) is an American moral philosopher, cultural commentator, and essayist. She has written extensively on the juncture between Enlightenment moral philosophy, metaphysics, and politics, both for scholarly audiences and the general public. She lives in Berlin.
