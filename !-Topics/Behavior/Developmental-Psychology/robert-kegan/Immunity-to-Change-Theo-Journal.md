## 2026-08-14

While on my after-breakfast outdoor walk, my brain made the connection between the levels of mental complexity and one of my favorite triads.

The socialized mind, the team mind, is something like eudemonia.
The self-authoring mind is like hedonism.
The Transformative Mind is like truth and beauty, psychological richness.

Another triad to add to my list of triads.

That's for myself. I like the thought that I can be any one of these things at any time. When it's about being on a team, I want to be a good team member. When it's about having good thoughts, I want to party. And when it's whatever, I want to be transformative, Beautiful and truthful. Or whatever.
