# Résumés Readme

Why do I like reading CVs and resumes?

Perhaps it is because I started like this as an architect, as a designer. As an architect, I created plans for the future. The C.V. is to assert a plan of the past. My job as an architect is to help my clients plan for the future using their CVs.

My plans are plans for the imagination of a structure of the future.

The CV is a plan of the imagination of what happened in the past.

So my role is that I look at a CV and help my client plan for their future based on what I read in the CV.

And just like an architect might do, I will look at the drawings and the plan and decide whether it is a handsome, beautiful document in and of itself.

As an architect, I like to look at architects' drawings. I like to see how handsome they are and how well they present their imagination.

Perhaps it is similar when I look at a resume. I like to look at its structure and its appearance and try to see if it's an accurate representation of the current reality.


How do I gauge a resume?

Perhaps some of it is the "everything visible" concept that I use elsewhere.

If I see something that is linkable, I want to see the link with the name of the company or university, for example.

I want the CV to be a table of contents for all sorts of things, not just in the resume. I want to click on things and see articles and drawings or portfolios.
