## Are You a Hugger? Your Answer May Be Telling.

How you remember an embrace could correlate with your attachment style, a new study says.

* https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/well/hugs-attachment-style-touch-survey.html


Self-touch is also healthy, said Tiffany Field, a professor in the departments of pediatrics and psychiatry at the University of Miami, who has researched touch for decades.

Studies have shown that “brushing” your skin or massaging yourself using hands or elbows can stimulate the skin’s pressure receptors, which starts a “cascade” of beneficial physical and mental effects, Dr. Field said.

“Everyone needs a daily dose of touch,” she added.
